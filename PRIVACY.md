# Privacy Policy — PureClip · 素笺

Last updated: 2026-11-26

**PureClip · 素笺** is a local-first, open-source web clipper extension.

## Data Collection & Storage
- **No Personal Data Collected or Transmitted**: PureClip does not collect, track, analyze, or transmit your browsing history, webpage contents, notes, or personal information to any developer server or third-party service.
- **Local-Only Processing**: Webpage extraction, sanitization, formatting, highlighting, and file export (HTML, Markdown, and print-to-PDF) run entirely locally inside your browser.
- **Local Storage**: Your typography preferences, custom category names, clipped URL history (for the duplicate-clip badge), and optional bound local directory handle are stored strictly on your device via browser extension storage and IndexedDB.
- **Network Requests**: The extension only fetches article images directly from their source URLs (using your existing browser session when necessary) to embed them into your offline saved files.

## User Control
You can view, export as JSON, or clear your local clip history and unbind local folders at any time inside the PureClip Manager page (`ui/manager.html`).
